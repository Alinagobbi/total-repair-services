What services does Total Repair Services offer?

We handle emergency disaster cleanup, tree removal and storm debris, demolition and debris removal, and site preparation for residential and commercial properties.

Where do you work?

We are a Florida-based company. Call or send us the property address and we will confirm that we can help.

How do I request emergency help?

The fastest way is to call (954) 579-0216. You can also fill out the form below with your address and a short description, and our team will get back to you.

What should I include in my request?

Your name, phone number, the property address, and a short description of what happened. Photos of the damage are helpful and can be uploaded in the form.

Can you help with a tarp or temporary roof protection?

Yes, our crews can secure damaged roofs with tarps to help limit further water intrusion. For full roof repair or rebuilding, contact us and we will talk through your situation.

Do you remove damaged materials from inside a home?

Yes. We remove damaged drywall, insulation and other compromised materials so the property is ready for the next stage of recovery.

How much does it cost?

Every property is different. Contact our team to describe your situation and we will walk you through the next steps.
